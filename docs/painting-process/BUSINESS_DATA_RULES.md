# Publish only verified business metrics

The approved image contains visually appealing sample figures **2013, 190+, 260+, 328+**. They are **not confirmed by the user**. Never publish those figures as a founding year, years of experience, completed projects, staff or customer satisfaction.

The Next.js component intentionally takes `metrics?: readonly ProcessMetric[]` and omits the statistics bar until exactly four real values have been provided by the site owner or a trusted CMS record.

When verified, pass metrics in this order:
1. Experience (actual relevant years and accurate label, not a founding year mislabeled as years)
2. Completed projects (verified count only)
3. Skilled professionals (actual number; a claim of 260 people needs supporting evidence)
4. Client satisfaction (percentage with defined basis; avoid unsubstantiated marketing numbers)

Do not invent values, manufacture testimonials or use exaggerated quality guarantees. The four metrics should be removed rather than replaced with mock numbers if the owner doesn't have confirmed data.

The standalone HTML preview displays example reference figures for visual measurement only and carries a visible disclaimer. It is NOT production site content.
