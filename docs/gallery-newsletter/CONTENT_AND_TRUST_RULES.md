# Content & Trust Rules
- AI-generated rooms shown in this section are illustrative visuals. Never claim they depict completed customer projects or actual property photography.
- The newsletter must not appear subscribed or send a fake email. A successful server response is required.
- A real Privacy Policy link is required for marketing email collection. Handle opt-in, retention and unsubscribe appropriately.
- FAQ text reflects the stated service menu, but must be checked against actual company practice before publishing. Avoid unsupported guarantees, numerical claims, licensing and dates.
- Do not use the AI mockup as a flattened screenshot in production. Text and controls must remain semantic and editable.
