## 360° vs static image

The approved artwork carries a baked 360° symbol. This alone does not create a 360° model, a panoramic virtual tour, or a WebGL experience. The photo is a static perspective illustration (not a full equirectangular capture). No viewer is shipped and none is claimed. Before a public launch, either supply genuine interactive 360 content and replace the art, or replace the stamped image with a clean static interior photograph to avoid misleading visitors.

## Company/portfolio claims

Never invent experience years, review stars, customer names, guarantees, completed project counts, or client details. The photographed room is AI-generated marketing artwork, not documentation of a finished job. It is suitable for presentation with a truthful alt, subject to final publication review.
